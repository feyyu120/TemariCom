# Decisions

## Decision 1 — Build a Unified Student Platform

### Decision

We decided to build TemariCom as a unified platform for students.

### Reason

Our student research showed that students use multiple platforms and
sources to find learning materials, internships, scholarships, tutors,
and university announcements.

Students reported using Telegram groups, social media, online platforms,
teachers, and official social media accounts to find different types
of information.

Based on these observations, we decided to explore a unified platform
that brings several student-focused resources and services together.

---

## Decision 2 — Start With a Web MVP

### Decision

We decided to build the initial TemariCom MVP as a web platform.

### Reason

A web application allows students to access the platform through a
browser without requiring a separate mobile application.

It also allows the team to develop and demonstrate the core features
within the available project time.

---

## Decision 3 — Prioritize Core Features

### Decision

The initial MVP will prioritize:

- learning resources
- internships, scholarships, and other opportunities
- tutors
- campus and university information
- community interaction
- lost & found
- chat

Other features will be considered after the core experience works.

### Reason

The complete TemariCom vision contains many different features and
services. Implementing all of them at once would make the project
larger and more technically complex.

Therefore, we decided to focus the MVP on the core features that are
most closely connected to the problems identified during our student
research.

---

## Decision 4 — Design for a Centralized Student Experience

### Decision

We decided to organize multiple student resources and services within
one platform rather than creating separate platforms for each need.

### Reason

Students reported using multiple sources to find different types of
information. They also reported difficulties when searching across
different websites and experiencing distractions from advertisements
and unrelated content.

TemariCom will therefore provide a more centralized place where
students can access different types of student-related information and
services.
