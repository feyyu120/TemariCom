# Alternatives

## Alternative 1 — Student Resource Website

Build a website that provides students with:

- learning materials
- scholarships
- internships
- university information
- campus information

### Advantages

- Simple to build
- Easy to organize and search
- Lower technical complexity
- Good for providing centralized information

### Limitations

- Limited student-to-student interaction
- No real-time communication
- Does not provide a strong student community
- Limited support for services such as delivery, marketplace, and lost
  & found

---

## Alternative 2 — Learning-Only Platform

Build a platform focused mainly on education, including:

- learning materials
- past exams
- notes
- educational videos
- tutors

### Advantages

- Clear and focused scope
- Easier to develop as an MVP
- Simple user experience
- Easier to manage educational content

### Limitations

- Does not address internships and scholarships
- Does not address campus navigation
- Does not provide a student marketplace
- Does not address lost and found
- Limited support for other campus-related services

---

## Alternative 3 — All-in-One Student Platform

Build a unified platform that combines:

- learning materials
- internships and scholarships
- tutors
- university and campus information
- campus maps and navigation
- student communication
- marketplace
- lost and found
- campus delivery services
- official announcements

### Advantages

- Addresses multiple related student needs in one platform
- Reduces the need to search across multiple platforms
- Provides a unified student experience
- Supports communication and student community
- Can connect educational resources with campus services and
  opportunities

### Limitations

- Larger project scope
- More technically complex
- Requires careful MVP selection
- Requires more content and user participation
- More features need to be tested and maintained

---

## Alternative Comparison

| Alternative                     | Main focus                                        | Advantages                                 | Limitations                                |
| :------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **Student Resource Website**    | Information and resources                         | Simple and easy to build                   | Limited interaction and services           |
| **Learning-Only Platform**      | Education                                         | Clear scope and easier MVP                 | Does not cover wider student needs         |
| **All-in-One Student Platform** | Education, opportunities, services, and community | Covers multiple student needs in one place | Larger scope and more technical complexity |

### Selected Direction

The **All-in-One Student Platform** was selected because it addresses the widest
range of student needs in one place. The initial MVP should focus on the most
important features first, while the remaining services can be added gradually.
