# Problem

## Problem Statement

Students often need to use multiple sources to find learning
materials,tutors, campus information,campus services(like delivery,reporting lost items ),scholarships, internships,
and other student-related services.

These resources may exist across different platforms, social media
pages, university channels, messaging groups, and websites.

This makes it difficult for students to discover relevant
information efficiently.

## Target Users

The primary users are Ethiopian students

## Problems Identified

- Learning resources can be difficult to discover.
- Finding tutors can be difficult.
- Campus-related information may be distributed across different
  sources.
- Campus services(like delivery, reporting lost items) may be difficult to find.
- Scholarship and internship opportunities can be scattered.
- Students may need several platforms for different activities.

## Why This Matters

Students spend time searching across different sources instead of
having a centralized place to discover relevant student resources
and opportunities.
