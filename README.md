# TemariCom

TemariCom is an all-in-one digital platform for Ethiopian students and young professionals. It brings learning resources,tutors, campus services, scholarships, internships, jobs, community interaction, and useful student services into one platform.

## How It Helps

TemariCom helps students and young professionals:

- Find learning materials and past exams
- Find and connect with tutors
- Access useful campus information and services(like delivery,bus schedules, and more)
- Report and find lost items
- Discover relevant opportunities and official announcements(from school/campus/government) in one place
- Discover scholarships, internships, jobs, and other opportunities
- Connect with other students and communities
- Buy and sell products through the marketplace
- Communicate through real-time chat

The goal is to reduce the fragmentation of student resources and opportunities by bringing them together in one accessible platform.
